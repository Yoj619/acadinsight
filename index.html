    KPI definitions, data sources and privacy
    Total enrolled
    Officially enrolled students in the program for the semester (Student and Faculty Portal).
    Average GWA
    Enrollment-weighted average of year-level general weighted averages. Lower is better.
    Withdrawal rate
    Students who dropped or withdrew ÷ total enrolled × 100.
    Class fill rate
    Total enrolled ÷ total capacity of the filtered sections × 100.
    Faculty below SETE threshold
    Faculty whose average SETE rating is below the alert level and whose response rate meets the minimum. Results with too few responses are not interpreted.

    Protecting confidential data

    • Faculty appear as codes (FAC-01 to FAC-08). Names are revealed only to authorized users after university sign-in.
    • Student data is shown only as totals and averages. No individual records or grades appear.
    • Role-based access: a Program Chair sees only their own program. Open-ended SETE comments are not displayed.
    • SETE results are one source of evidence. They are read together with peer observation, class outcomes and context.
